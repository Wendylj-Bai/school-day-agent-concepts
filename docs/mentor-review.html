Concept 1 · Consolidated learning direction

School-Guided Learning Support

Help students plan and practice without turning AI into an answer machine, while giving authorized adults an appropriate view of progress.

Shared problem

General AI may operate outside the teacher's curriculum, reveal answers instead of teaching, and provide little appropriate visibility into how the learner progressed. Students may also struggle to organize preparation or find suitable academic support.

Three team contributions being consolidated

Detailed public brief

Personalized Study Buddy

Reviews a practice attempt, chooses a teacher-approved hint, checks a fresh question, adjusts within limits, and uses a no-hint check before sharing role-appropriate progress summaries.

Internal team submission

Teacher's Assistant AI

Lets a teacher set the acceptable AI-use level, coaches rather than rewrites, records how AI was used, and leaves every academic decision with the teacher.

What the demo proves

The workflow follows approved learning boundaries, changes support after a checked result, and reconnects the learner with an authorized adult.

Keep outside the first prototype

Tutor marketplace, payments, campus forecasting, complete parent and teacher portals, misconduct judgments, class analytics, and unnecessary multi-agent complexity.

Concept 2 · Family-school direction

Family-School Communication Coordinator

Help families understand approved school information while keeping educators responsible for unresolved or sensitive questions.

Problem

Families may receive information through separate notices, emails, messages, calendars, and verbal updates. Busy parents can miss changes or encounter incomplete or apparently conflicting details, while educators repeat information or correct misunderstandings.

Small four-week demonstration

Parent asks about conflicting notices
Permission is checked
Approved sources are compared
Agent explains or requests clarification
Educator confirms the answer

Proposed response

Retrieve only approved information, compare sources and dates, provide a cited answer when evidence is clear, and ask an authorized educator when it is not. The agent never guesses.

Review full Family-School details →

Keep outside the first prototype

Replacing existing school systems, unsupervised mass messaging, real student records, real family data, and simultaneous integration with every communication channel.
